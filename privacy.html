Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Life OS is a personal tool used only by its developer, on their own accounts.

  • What it accesses: the developer's own Gmail messages (read-only) and Google Calendar, through Google's official APIs and only after the account owner grants consent.
  • Where data goes: it is stored only on the developer's own computer. It is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Sending: Life OS cannot send email. It may create drafts and calendar events only when the account owner approves them.
  • Google API data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and local data is deleted by removing the tool's folder.
